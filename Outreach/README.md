# Outreach

Talks given outside the course. Everything here is public and free to reuse.

### Was the Universe an Accident?

A 20-minute talk for high school juniors and seniors in a year-long research class: how you get
from a high school classroom to the Large Hadron Collider, what the LHC's data problem actually
looks like, why that problem turns into artificial intelligence, and why scientists make good data
scientists.

If you saw this talk, the things mentioned in it are in this repository:

- `Chapters/Chapter.01.Computers.pdf` — how computers work
- `Chapters/Chapter.03.Probability.and.Statistics.pdf` — uncertainty, distributions, error bars
- `Lectures/Lecture.13/Lecture.13.Slides.pdf` — how to look at data, which is the useful part for a
  science project
- `Lectures/Lecture.9/Lecture.9.Standard-Model-Shirt.pdf` — the t-shirt, one term at a time
- `Labs/Lab.1/` and `Labs/GitHub-Setup.pdf` — how to set a laptop up to do any of this

The degree this talk describes: <https://www.uta.edu/science/data-science/>
